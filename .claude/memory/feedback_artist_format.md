All bingo rounds should default to artist names on the cards, not song titles.

**Why:** When cards show artist names, Nick can swap in a different song by the same artist on the fly during hosting if he disagrees with a pick or reads the room. Song-title cards lock him into a specific track.

**How to apply:** Default every round to artist format. Only use song titles for rounds where the artist is genuinely unrecognizable to the crowd (e.g., One-Hit Wonders). When creating `_spotify_ref.txt` files, always include the specific song to search for on Spotify.
