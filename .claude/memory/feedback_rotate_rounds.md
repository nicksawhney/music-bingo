Nick tracks how recently a round ran. As of 2026-08-12, Guilty Pleasures had run ~3
nights in a row and Nick asked to swap it out (replaced with Throwback Summer Jams).

**But on 2026-08-25, offered the choice between refreshing repeat rounds or keeping
them identical, Nick chose "keep them identical"** — Nickelodeon & Disney ran the
same 26-song list for a third straight week, and Famous Intros reran the 5/20 + 8/12
classic-rock list unchanged.

**On 2026-09-02** Nick rested Nickelodeon & Disney after its 4th-week-running would
have been due, but drew an explicit exception for Pop Divas: *"always a fun round and
never gets old."* Pop Divas can run every single week without resting; themed
nostalgia rounds cannot.

**Why:** Staleness is a real risk, but re-curating a proven round costs effort and
risks a worse list. Which matters more depends on the night — a crowd of first-timers
makes reruns free. It's Nick's call, not an automatic refresh.

**How to apply:** Do check recent `playlists/` folders and *surface* repeats explicitly
("this ran identically on 8/12 and 8/19"), with a refresh option. Then build whatever
Nick picks. Don't silently re-curate. Exception: don't bother flagging Pop Divas as
stale. Also expect Nick to redirect mid-plan — he swaps rounds out by feel once he sees
the lineup, so confirm the shape before building playlists. See [[feedback_round_energy]]
and [[feedback_no_song_overlap]].
