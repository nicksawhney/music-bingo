On 10/28/2026 I proposed dropping *Witch Doctor* (David Seville) to make room for a
requested song, on the reasoning that it was dated. Nick overruled it immediately
("Noooo keep witch doctor drop something else"). The replacement cut was *Shout at the
Devil* — one of four devil songs in the same round.

**Why:** the novelty/goofy track is the one that gets a reaction in the room. A fourth
near-duplicate of a serious theme is the actually-expendable item. I was optimizing for
"is this song dated" when the real axis is "does this song do something none of the
others do."

**How to apply:** when a round needs to lose an item, cut for *redundancy* first — the
Nth song on the same sub-theme — not for age or perceived cheesiness. Flag the proposed
cut before making it. Related: [[feedback-round-energy]], [[feedback-tight-round-frames]].
