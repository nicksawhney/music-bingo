On 2026-08-25 a friend (Aeslyn, a WNYU college-radio DJ) contributed two playlists for
bingo night. Spotify blocked reading them, and I offered to curate equivalents from the
theme names. Nick declined: *"you're prone to errors if you try to recreate it and I want
to have exactly what she set up."*

**Why:** A guest's playlist is their creative work — the sequencing and deep cuts are the
point of them contributing. An approximation reproduces the label, not the set, and quietly
takes credit away from the contributor.

**How to apply:** When an external person's content is blocked or unavailable, get the real
thing — ask Nick to duplicate/export it, or to paste it in. Offer reconstruction only as an
explicitly-labeled last resort. Applies beyond playlists: any time the ask references
something specific that someone else made. See [[reference_spotify_guest_playlists]].
