A round theme must be a **tight frame with a point of view**, not a mood. Nick rejected a
"Road Trip Songs" list on 9/16/2026 as "all over the place and very AI-feeling."

**Why:** Vibe themes ("road trip", "feel good", "songs that slap") have no boundary, so the
list gets filled by checking genre boxes — one country, one Latin, one emo, one indie — and
by pulling songs that are only on-theme via movie association (Life Is a Highway/*Cars*,
Come and Get Your Love/*Guardians*, Tiny Dancer/*Almost Famous*). That reproduces the first
page of a "50 Best ___ Songs" listicle. Era sprawl (1971→2015 with no logic) is the other tell.

**How to apply:** Prefer **identity rounds** — the title should make someone in the room go
"that's me." Scorned Women, Millennials in Love, Nickelodeon & Disney all pass. Anchor each
round to a tight era and/or a specific scene so the songs cohere as a set. If a theme can't
be bounded, it's not a round. See [[feedback_round_energy]].
