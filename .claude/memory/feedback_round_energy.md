Rounds that **fall flat** — do not reuse without Nick asking for them by name:
- **Guilty Pleasures** (as of 9/16/2026)
- **Party Anthems** (as of 9/16/2026)
- **Songs With a Name** (as of 2/25/2026)

Rounds that **land**: Scorned Women, Pop Divas, R&B Hits, Nickelodeon & Disney,
NYC Anthems — the ones Nick re-runs week after week.

**Why:** The winners share emotional investment + singability. The flops are either
trivia-shaped (Songs With a Name) or novelty/club-hype that a seated pub crowd
won't dance to (Party Anthems, Guilty Pleasures). This pub sings; it doesn't rave.

**How to apply:** Favor rounds the crowd can belt with feeling over ironic-kitsch or
DJ/club bangers. Note this reversed an earlier belief that Party Anthems and Guilty
Pleasures were crowd favorites — Nick corrected it directly. See [[feedback_rotate_rounds]].
