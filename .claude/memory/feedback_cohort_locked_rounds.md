On 2026-11-11 Nick cut R&B Hits: "R&B has been hard for people lately. It really depends
on the demographic that shows up on a given night." NOTES.md still called it a reliable
crowd-pleaser — that entry is now marked superseded.

**Why:** the R&B Hits list is 90s/2000s-locked (TLC, Blackstreet, Ginuwine), so it only
lands for a mid-30s–40s room and dies outside it. The failure isn't the genre, it's that
the round is pinned to one cohort. The same risk applies to Millennials in Love, Hip Hop &
Rap, 2000s Bangers and Gen Z Party Anthems — Nick still likes several of those, so don't
treat this as a ban.

**How to apply:** when the crowd is unpredictable, favour rounds that span decades *by
construction* — Super Bowl Halftime (booked on broad fame), One-Hit Wonders (radio
saturation), Karaoke Classics. When Nick picks several cohort-locked rounds anyway, that's
his call; just don't stack them without noting it. Relates to [[feedback-round-energy]]
and [[feedback-rotate-rounds]].
