## R2 Blue Tsunami

Artists from the seven states whose Senate seats flipped — TX, OH, NC, SC, KS, IA, ME.
Card shows artist names only, so **the conceit only exists when you say it out loud.**
Announce it or the round is just a good mixed-artist round.

Spread: Texas 9, Ohio 8, North Carolina 5, South Carolina 3, Kansas 3, Iowa 1, Maine 1.

Three things worth knowing on the mic:

- **Ella Langley is from Alabama**, not a flipped state. She's in because *Choosin' Texas*
  spent 27 weeks at #1 and is literally called *Choosin' Texas*. If anyone presses:
  **Miranda Lambert co-wrote and co-produced it, and she's from Longview, Texas.**
- **Kansas** (the band) representing Kansas, and **Slipknot** as the only famous act Iowa
  has produced, are both free jokes. Use them.
- **James Taylor → *Carolina in My Mind*** landed by accident and is perfect for NC.

Beyoncé, Lizzo, Destiny's Child and John Legend all qualify geographically but were
excluded — they're already in other rounds tonight.

## R6 Holiday — BLACKOUT

Mid-November, so this is the season's first. Blackout means every song gets played, which
is why the list is weighted hard toward instant recognition — 21 of 30 are the undeniable
canon.

Nick's calls: Tyler, The Creator's *You're a Mean One, Mr. Grinch* from the 2018 movie;
*NSYNC and Kanye added for modern weight; Dean Martin for *Baby, It's Cold Outside*.

**Cut and why:**

- **Fairytale of New York** — the original's third verse has the f-slur. It was also the
  lowest US recognition on the list, so cutting it solved both problems at once.
- **8 Days of Christmas** (Destiny's Child) — 2001 album track, too deep for a blackout.
- **Someday at Christmas**, **Give Love on Christmas Day**, **Merry Christmas Baby** — cut
  to make room for modern. *This Christmas* and *Silent Night* kept as the two strongest
  soul records.

**Weakest link to watch:** *Christmas In Harlem* (Kanye, 2010 GOOD Friday release, never
charted). Nick asked for it by name. If the room goes blank, that's why — log it.
